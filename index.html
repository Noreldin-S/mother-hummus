Four steps, start to lid

Most of this
is just waiting.

STEP 01 — SOAK

Eighteen hours, cold

Dry Kabuli chickpeas go into cold water the night before with a pinch of baking soda to soften the skins.

18 h · 4 °C · 1:3 bean to water

STEP 02 — PEEL

Every skin comes off

The slipped skins get rubbed away by hand before the cook. It's the slowest part of the day and the reason the texture is what it is.

~40 min per 20 kg batch

STEP 03 — MILL

Tahini, ground on stone

Hulled sesame roasted light and milled between stones, so it stays loose and pourable instead of turning bitter.

Roast 118 °C · single-pass stone mill

STEP 04 — WHIP

Ice water, long blend

Iced water goes in slowly at the end. It keeps the friction heat down and takes the hummus pale and airy.

7 min · finish below 20 °C
